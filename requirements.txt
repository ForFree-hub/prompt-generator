**Step 2: Deploy to Render**

1. Go to [render.com](https://render.com) → Sign in with GitHub
2. Click **"New Web Service"**
3. Search for your repo: `prompt-generator`
4. Click **"Connect"**
5. Configure:
   - **Name:** `prompt-generator` (or whatever you want)
   - **Build Command:** `pip install -r requirements.txt`
   - **Start Command:** `python app.py`
6. Scroll down, click **"Create Web Service"**

---

**Step 3: Wait for Deploy**

- It will install packages and start your app
- Takes about 1-2 minutes ⏳
- When done, you'll see a **"Your service is live"** message 🎉

---

**Step 4: Visit Your Live App!**

Click the URL Render gives you (something like `https://prompt-generator.onrender.com`)

---

**Done!** Let me know if you get any errors during deployment and I'll help you fix them! 🚀
